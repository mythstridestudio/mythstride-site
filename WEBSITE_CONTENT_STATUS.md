## Preserved product and lore promises

The public experience continues to present Elyndor, bosses, raids, sagas,
inventory, loot, diamonds, Aethron, events, friends, groups, rankings,
achievements, the Founder Sword, Wear OS, Strava, future iOS support and future
monetization.

Lore is visually separated from factual product explanations. “Aethron has
awakened” is a narrative statement; availability is communicated by the
validation badge and supporting disclosure.

## Wording boundaries

- No public download or release date is promised.
- Joining the beta list records interest and does not guarantee an invitation.
- No health, recovery or performance outcome is guaranteed.
- Diamonds have no real-world cash value and cannot be withdrawn.
- Future roadmap items may change based on testing.
- Draft legal and support text is not an approved policy.
